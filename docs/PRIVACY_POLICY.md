## The short version
- GolfCaddie works **without an account**. Your profile, bag, rounds, stats and caddie conversations are stored **on your iPhone**, plus your own iCloud or device backups.
- **Friends is optional.** If you use it, your name, handicap index, scoring average, number of rounds played and your friend code are stored in Apple iCloud (CloudKit), so friends you share your code with can see them. By default your completed rounds (course, date, tees and hole-by-hole scores) are shared the same way, and you can turn that off any time in Friends. Nothing else leaves your phone.
- We **don't sell** your data, **don't show ads**, and **don't track** you across other apps or websites.
- The **AI caddie runs entirely on your iPhone** (Apple Intelligence where available, otherwise GolfCaddie's built-in caddie). Your conversations are never sent to us or to any AI company.

## What the app uses, and why
| Data | Where it goes | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Precise location** (only while the app is in use) | Stays on your device. Apple receives it to return local weather (WeatherKit) and search results (Apple Maps). Course coordinates, not your position, are sent to OpenStreetMap's Overpass service to load hole maps. Two map points (your position and your target) are sent to Open-Meteo to look up terrain elevation. | Distances, "plays like" numbers, course search |
| **Microphone & speech** (only while you hold the talk button) | Converted to text on your device when your iPhone supports it; otherwise by Apple's speech service. No audio is sent to us. | Talking to your caddie |
| **Course maps** | When you open a course, GolfCaddie asks GolfCaddie's own map files (hosted at stevenrbrown.org) for it, and the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service if they don't have it. The map host sees which map area and course you opened and your IP address, like any website. It does not receive your name, profile or location. | Loading the course's hole maps quickly |
| **Green slope** | When you open a green's slope view, the green's own coordinates (not your name or position) are sent to the U.S. Geological Survey's public elevation service to get its heights. The result is saved on your phone. | Showing which way a green tilts |
| **Profile, bag, rounds, scores, shots, conversations** | Stored on your device only | Your caddie advice, stats and history |
| **Profile photo** (optional) | You choose one photo with Apple's photo picker; the app never gets access to the rest of your library. A small copy is stored on your device only | Shown on your profile |
| **Caddie conversations** | Processed on your device only (Apple Foundation Models / GolfCaddie's engine) | Caddie replies |
| **Friends (optional):** name, handicap index, scoring average, rounds played, friend code, friend requests, and (unless you turn it off in Friends) your completed rounds: course name, date, tees and hole-by-hole par, strokes and putts. Never your notes, tracked shots, GPS positions or playing partners | Stored in Apple iCloud (CloudKit), in GolfCaddie's public database for the profile and in your own private iCloud for your friend list. Visible to people who have your friend code. We do not operate a separate server and cannot read your private iCloud. | Letting friends compare handicaps, see each other's rounds, and send or accept friend requests |
| **Notifications (optional)** | Delivered by Apple's push service when someone sends you a friend request. Only a friend request notification is sent. | Telling you when someone adds you |

### We don't operate servers
GolfCaddie has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own. If you use Friends, that data is stored with Apple iCloud, and you can remove it any time with *Delete all my data*, which also deletes your public Friends profile and shared rounds. Turning off "Share my rounds with friends" removes your shared rounds from iCloud straight away.

## Your choices and rights
- **Delete everything:** Settings ▸ *Delete all my data* permanently erases all GolfCaddie data from your device.
- **Export:** Settings ▸ *Export my data* gives you a copy (JSON).
- **Permissions:** you can turn location, microphone, speech and notifications on or off in iOS Settings at any time. The app still works without them, with fewer features.
- **California (CCPA/CPRA), EU/UK (GDPR), and other US state laws:** you have the right to know, access, delete and correct your data, and to opt out of "sale" or "sharing". We don't sell or share personal information. Because we hold no account data on our servers, most requests are handled by the in-app controls above. For anything else, email us and we'll respond within 30 days (45 days where the law allows).
- **Legal basis (GDPR):** any processing happens on your device to provide the app's features.

## Children
GolfCaddie isn't directed to children under 13, and users confirm they're 13 or older during setup. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and Friends is only for golfers who are 13 or older. If you believe a child under 13 has used Friends, email us and we'll delete their data.

## Security
Data on your device is protected by iOS file encryption (Data Protection). Secrets are kept in the iOS Keychain. All network traffic uses HTTPS.
